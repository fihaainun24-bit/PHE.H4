Pertamina Strengthens Its Upstream Oil and Gas Portfolio Through the Acquisition of Eni's Participating Interests in East Kalimantan Blocks

PT Pertamina Hulu Energi (PHE) has successfully acquired Eni's Participating Interests (PI) in three strategic oil and gas blocks in East Kalimantan: North Ganal, Rapak, and East Sepinggan. PHE now holds a 10% PI in North Ganal and Rapak, and increased its PI in East Sepinggan from 15% to 25%. The transaction strengthens Pertamina's position in a key gas production and deepwater development hub.
